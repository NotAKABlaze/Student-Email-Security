# AI-Use Declaration

In accordance with project integrity guidelines, this document outlines the use of Generative AI during the development of this framework.

## Scope of AI Assistance
Generative AI tools were utilized to assist with project management and documentation structuring. Specifically, Claude and Google Gemini assisted with drafting the stakeholder analysis, literature synthesis, reference formatting, and structuring the milestone's evidence logs.

## Verification & Human Oversight
No AI-generated outputs were integrated without strict human verification. The verification process ensured that the exact prompts used were saved, noted by the team members, and verified against the academic sources used. All code, including the plain Python tool-calling loop and the deterministic rule-based permission gate, was manually engineered to ensure complete control over the execution path and to maintain statistical stability.
